# Initial Database of Recipes and Products

## 1. Introduction

This document defines the initial set of products and recipes for the intelligent meal planning platform. The data is intended to support early development, testing of the recipe generation module, and demonstration of basic scenarios involving available products, dietary preferences, and budget.

## 3. Recipes

### R01 — Chicken Rice Bowl
- **Ingredients:** Chicken breast (300 g), Rice (200 g), Carrot (100 g), Onion (50 g), Oil (20 ml), Salt, Pepper
- **Steps:**
  1. Cook rice.
  2. Fry chicken with onion and carrot.
  3. Combine and season.
- **Servings:** 2
- **Estimated cost:** ~1200 KZT
- **Tags:** high-protein, quick

### R02 — Pasta with Tomato and Garlic
- **Ingredients:** Pasta (200 g), Tomato (300 g), Garlic (20 g), Oil (20 ml), Salt
- **Steps:**
  1. Boil pasta.
  2. Fry garlic and tomato.
  3. Mix with pasta.
- **Servings:** 2
- **Estimated cost:** ~700 KZT
- **Tags:** vegetarian, quick

### R03 — Potato and Egg Breakfast
- **Ingredients:** Potatoes (400 g), Eggs (3 pcs), Onion (50 g), Oil (20 ml), Salt
- **Steps:**
  1. Fry potatoes with onion.
  2. Add eggs and cook.
- **Servings:** 2
- **Estimated cost:** ~400 KZT
- **Tags:** breakfast, budget

### R04 — Beef Stew
- **Ingredients:** Beef (400 g), Potatoes (300 g), Carrot (150 g), Onion (100 g), Oil, Salt, Pepper
- **Steps:**
  1. Brown the beef.
  2. Add vegetables and simmer.
- **Servings:** 3
- **Estimated cost:** ~1800 KZT
- **Tags:** dinner, hearty

### R05 — Vegetable Omelette
- **Ingredients:** Eggs (4 pcs), Tomato (150 g), Onion (50 g), Oil (15 ml), Salt
- **Steps:**
  1. Fry vegetables.
  2. Add beaten eggs and cook.
- **Servings:** 2
- **Estimated cost:** ~500 KZT
- **Tags:** vegetarian, breakfast

## 4. Notes

- Prices are approximate and used for budget-related testing.
- The initial set is intentionally small and will be expanded during development.
- Recipes are designed so that they can be matched against available products and filtered by preferences and budget.
